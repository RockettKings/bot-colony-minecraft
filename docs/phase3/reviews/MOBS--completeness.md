| 1 | blocker | §5 and §6 | No rule for when no tactic of an entry is eligible (all conditions false). The bot would have no action. | Add to §5 intro: "If no tactic in an entry's `tactics` list has all conditions true, the executor uses the first of: `melee_strafe` if the bot has a weapon; otherwise `sprint_away`." | 2 | major | §1.5 CONDITION grammar | There is no table saying how each atom is evaluated (source field in `Percept`/`EntityPercept`, units, comparison).
