# Review: MOBS — precision
Skipped as already decided: D15 (fallback when no tactic's `when` holds), D18 (spider light and daylight), D19 (evoker id `minecraft:evocation_illager`), D2 (terrain atoms in the Percept).

| # | Severity | Location | Problem | Exact fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | blocker | §1.5 CONDITION grammar vs §3 | Internal contradiction. §1.5 says a condition is "one of these atoms, or a conjunction written `a AND b`", but §3 uses `OR` three times (drowned `mob_in_water OR bot_in_water`, creeper `not_mob_aggroed_on_bot OR mob_charged`, phantom `hostile_count_at_least: 3 OR hp_below: 10`) and `flee_if` items such as `bot_in_water AND hp_below: 12`. A builder following §1.5 literally rejects the data. | Replace the first two sentences of §1.5 with: "A condition is a list of terms joined by `AND` and `OR`. `AND` binds tighter than `OR`. There are no parentheses. A term is an atom, optionally prefixed `not_` (boolean atoms only) and optionally followed by `: N` (numeric atoms only). Examples: `not_mob_is_baby AND ground_flat AND count_at_least: 1`; `mob_in_water OR bot_in_water`." Keep "No other atoms are allowed." |
| 2 | major | new atoms `mob_size_large`, `mob_size_medium`, `mob_size_small` (§1.5, slime §3, magma_cube §3) | The atoms are never defined: nothing says how the bot reads a slime's size. S1 `EntityPercept` and S3 `MobView` have only an `aabb`. | Add to §1.5: "`mob_size_*` is derived from the AABB width `w = 2 * aabb.extent.x` of the target: `mob_size_large` if `w >= 1.5`, `mob_size_medium` if `0.8 <= w < 1.5`, `mob_size_small` if `w < 0.8` (slime/magma cube size 4, 2, 1 have widths of about 2.04, 1.02, 0.51). The atoms are false for every other mob." Add the same sentence to S3 §3.1 as the `MobView` rule. |
| 3 | major | §5 `retreat_and_regen` step 4 | "`hp_at_least` the central config's re-engage threshold" names no config key. | Replace with "`hp_at_least: config.combat.recoverHp` (16 HP)". |
